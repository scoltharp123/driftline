Rivers

Each river has its USGS gauge, a rating for the current flow, a hatch chart, flies, access, regulations and nearby shops. You can add any USGS gauge in the country.

Add a river from any USGS gauge

Find the 8 to 15 digit site number on waterdata.usgs.gov. Set the ideal flow range from local knowledge so Driftline can rate it.

Hatch calendar

Darker cells mean a heavier hatch. The outlined column is this month. Water temperature moves hatches by weeks, so use it as a guide and read the water.

Fly box recommender

Pick the river and conditions. Driftline weighs the hatch calendar against water temperature, sky and time of day, then suggests dries, nymphs and a streamer with the right tippet.
